2. What the valuations are — and are not

Trove estimates what an item is worth by applying a decay curve to its original price, using parameters derived from typical second-hand market behaviour for its category and, where available, its specific model. This is a model. It cannot see your item's actual condition, its service history, local market conditions, or the current demand for it.

长物的估值方式，是在物品原价上套用一条衰减曲线，参数来自该品类——以及在有数据时，该具体型号——在二手市场上的典型表现。这是一个模型。它看不到你这件东西的实际状况、维修历史、当地行情，也看不到眼下的供需。

Actual sale prices will differ, sometimes substantially. The calibration feature exists precisely because the model needs correcting against reality. Treat every number in the app as a starting point for your own judgement, not a substitute for it.
