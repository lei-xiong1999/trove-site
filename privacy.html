Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective 1 August 2026 · Applies to Trove (长物) for iOS

生效日期 2026 年 8 月 1 日 · 适用于 iOS 版长物（Trove）

The short version: Trove collects nothing. It has no account system, no servers, and no networking code of any kind. Everything you record stays in the app's private storage on your device, and the developer has no way to see it.

一句话版本：长物不收集任何信息。它没有账号系统，没有服务器，也完全没有任何联网代码。你记录的一切都留在设备上这个 app 的私有存储里，开发者没有任何途径看到它。

1. What we collect

一、我们收集什么

Nothing. Trove does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. This is not a policy choice we could quietly reverse — the app contains no networking code, so there is no path by which data could leave your device.

什么都不收集。长物不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何个人信息。这不是一句可以悄悄反悔的承诺——这个 app 里没有任何联网代码，数据在技术上就没有离开你设备的路径。

2. What you enter, and where it lives

二、你输入的内容存在哪里

When you record an item you may enter a name, a price, a purchase date, a category, and how heavily you use it. This is written to a single file inside the app's own container on your device (the system's Application Support directory). It is never uploaded anywhere.

你记录一件物品时，可能会输入名称、价格、购买日期、类型和使用强度。这些内容会写入设备上属于这个 app 的容器里的一个文件（系统的 Application Support 目录）。它不会被上传到任何地方。

Product reference data — the model catalogue and the exchange-rate table — is bundled inside the app itself. Nothing is looked up online.

参考数据——型号目录和汇率表——是打包在 app 内部的，不会去网上查询任何东西。

3. Device backups

三、设备备份

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups enabled, your device backup may include Trove's data file, in the same way it includes data from your other apps. That is Apple's mechanism, governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to your backups.

如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或加密的本地备份，设备备份中可能包含长物的数据文件——和你其他 app 的数据一样。这是苹果的机制，适用苹果的隐私政策。开发者无法访问你的备份。

4. Third parties

四、第三方

There are none. Trove contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no attribution SDK, and no third-party libraries of any kind.

没有任何第三方。长物里没有数据分析、没有崩溃上报、没有广告、没有归因 SDK，也没有任何第三方库。

5. Permissions

五、权限

Trove requests no system permissions. It does not access your camera, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, health data, or notifications. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

长物不申请任何系统权限。它不访问你的相机、相册、定位、通讯录、麦克风、健康数据或通知。它不使用广告标识符，也不会跨 app 或网站追踪你。

The one place the app opens something outside itself is the Language row in Settings, which opens the iOS Settings page for Trove. No information is passed along.

app 里唯一一处会打开外部内容的地方是设置页的「语言」一行，它会跳到 iOS 系统设置中长物的页面。这个过程不传递任何信息。

6. Your control over the data

六、你对数据的控制

  • Delete individual items from the item detail screen.
  • Use Settings → Restore sample data to overwrite everything you have recorded.
  • Delete the app from your device to remove all of its data permanently.
  • 在物品详情页删除单件物品。
  • 用「设置 → 恢复示例数据」覆盖掉你记录的全部内容。
  • 从设备上删除 app，即可永久清除它的所有数据。

Because no copy of your data exists anywhere else, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf and no request process to go through.

因为你的数据在别处不存在任何副本，所以开发者这边没有东西可以替你删除，你也不需要走任何申请流程。

7. Children

七、儿童

Trove is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

长物不面向儿童，也不从任何人收集信息——包括 13 岁以下（或你所在地区规定的相应年龄）的儿童。

8. Your rights

八、你的权利

Regulations such as the GDPR, the CCPA, and China's PIPL grant you rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability over personal data held by a company. Trove holds none: you are the only party with a copy of your data, and it sits on your own device under your own control. The developer processes no personal data and therefore acts as neither a controller nor a processor with respect to your records.

GDPR、CCPA、中国《个人信息保护法》等法规赋予你对企业所持个人数据的访问、更正、删除和可携带权。长物不持有任何这类数据：你是唯一持有自己数据副本的一方，它就在你自己的设备上、由你自己掌控。开发者不处理任何个人信息，因此对你的记录既不构成个人信息处理者，也不构成受托方。

9. Changes to this policy

九、政策变更

Future versions may add optional features that need a network connection — for example looking up a second-hand price. If that ever happens, this page will be updated to describe exactly what is sent and when, before the feature ships, and the effective date at the top will change. Any such feature will be opt-in.

未来的版本可能会加入需要联网的可选功能——比如查询二手成交价。真的发生时，本页会在功能上线之前更新，明确写清楚什么时候发送了什么，顶部的生效日期也会随之改变。这类功能都会是需要你主动开启的。
