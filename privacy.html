Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective 3 August 2026 · Applies to Trove (长物) for iOS

生效日期 2026 年 8 月 3 日 · 适用于 iOS 版长物（Trove）

The short version: nothing you record leaves your device. Trove has no account system and no server of its own, and it sends none of your data anywhere. The only network traffic the app generates is Apple's own purchase flow, which never carries your records.

一句话版本：你记录的任何内容都不会离开你的设备。长物没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器，不向任何地方发送你的数据。app 产生的唯一网络流量是苹果自己的购买流程，那里面不含你的任何记录。

1. What we collect

一、我们收集什么

Nothing. There is no server behind Trove and no account to sign into, so there is nowhere for your records to be sent and nothing for the developer to look at. Your list of items, their prices, dates and notes never leave your device.

什么都不收集。长物背后没有服务器，也没有账号可登录，所以你的记录无处可发，开发者也无从查看。你的物品清单、价格、日期和备注都不会离开你的设备。

Item names, prices, dates and notes are never transmitted — not to the developer, and not to any third party. Category recognition, the model catalogue and the exchange-rate table all run entirely offline from data bundled inside the app.

物品名称、价格、日期和备注从不被传输——不发给开发者，也不发给任何第三方。品类识别、型号目录和汇率表全部离线运行，数据打包在 app 内部。

2. What you enter, and where it lives

二、你输入的内容存在哪里

When you record an item you may enter a name, a price, a purchase date, a category, and how heavily you use it. This is written to a single file inside the app's own container on your device (the system's Application Support directory). It is never uploaded anywhere.

你记录一件物品时，可能会输入名称、价格、购买日期、类型和使用强度。这些内容会写入设备上属于这个 app 的容器里的一个文件（系统的 Application Support 目录）。它不会被上传到任何地方。

Product reference data — the model catalogue and the exchange-rate table — is bundled inside the app itself. Nothing is looked up online.

参考数据——型号目录和汇率表——是打包在 app 内部的，不会向网络查询任何内容。

3. Purchases

三、购买

Trove offers a one-time purchase that removes the free item limit. It is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; the app only asks the system whether the purchase exists. The developer never sees your payment details, your Apple ID, or your purchase history, and no purchase information is stored on any server.

长物提供一次性内购以解除免费额度限制。整个流程由苹果通过 App Store 处理，app 只向系统查询「这笔购买是否存在」。开发者看不到你的支付信息、Apple ID 或购买记录，也没有任何服务器保存购买信息。

4. Device backups

四、设备备份

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups enabled, your device backup may include Trove's data file, in the same way it includes data from your other apps. That is Apple's mechanism, governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to your backups.

如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或加密的本地备份，设备备份中可能包含长物的数据文件——和你其他 app 的数据一样。这是苹果的机制，适用苹果的隐私政策。开发者无法访问你的备份。

5. Third parties

五、第三方

Trove contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no attribution SDK, and no third-party libraries of any kind. Nothing profiles you and nothing follows you between apps.

长物里没有数据分析、没有崩溃上报、没有广告、没有归因 SDK，也没有任何第三方库。不存在任何用户画像，也不会跨 app 追踪你。

The only outside party involved at all is Apple, which handles purchases (section 3). Apple is not an advertising or analytics partner here, and it never receives your records.

全部涉及的外部方只有苹果一个，负责处理购买（第三节）。它在这里不是广告或分析伙伴，也拿不到你的任何记录。

6. Permissions

六、权限

Trove requests no system permissions. It does not access your camera, photo library, location, contacts, microphone, health data, or notifications. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

长物不申请任何系统权限。它不访问你的相机、相册、定位、通讯录、麦克风、健康数据或通知。它不使用广告标识符，也不会跨 app 或网站追踪你。

The Language row in Settings opens the iOS Settings page for Trove; no information is passed along. Links to this website open in your browser.

设置页的「语言」一行会跳到 iOS 系统设置中长物的页面，这个过程不传递任何信息。指向本网站的链接会在浏览器中打开。

7. Your control over the data

七、你对数据的控制

  • Delete individual items from the item detail screen.
  • Use Settings → Restore sample data to overwrite everything you have recorded.
  • Delete the app from your device to remove all of its data permanently.
  • 在物品详情页删除单件物品。
  • 用「设置 → 恢复示例数据」覆盖掉你记录的全部内容。
  • 从设备上删除 app，即可永久清除它的所有数据。

Because no copy of your data exists anywhere else, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf and no request process to go through.

由于你的数据在别处不存在任何副本，开发者无从代为删除，你也无需走任何申请流程。

8. Children

八、儿童

Trove is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

长物不面向儿童，也不从任何人收集信息——包括 13 岁以下（或你所在地区规定的相应年龄）的儿童。

9. Your rights

九、你的权利

Regulations such as the GDPR, the CCPA, and China's PIPL grant you rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability over personal data a company holds about you. The developer holds none — your records exist only on your device, under your control, and there is no server-side copy to request, export, or erase.

GDPR、CCPA、中国《个人信息保护法》等法规赋予你对企业所持个人数据的访问、更正、删除和可携带权。开发者不持有任何这类数据——你的记录只存在于你自己的设备上、由你掌控，不存在任何服务器端副本可供调取、导出或删除。

For purchases, Apple is the party processing the transaction under its own policy.

对于购买，处理交易的一方是苹果，适用其自身政策。

10. Changes to this policy

十、政策变更

A pre-release build briefly sent unrecognised item names to a third-party encyclopaedia to guess a category. That was removed before release: recognition now runs entirely on device. If any future version sends something off the device, this page will be updated to say exactly what and when, before that version ships.

有一个未发布的开发版本曾经把认不出的物品名称发给第三方百科去猜品类。该功能在发布前已被移除，识别现在完全在设备上完成。若未来某个版本会将任何数据发送出设备，本页将在该版本发布之前写明发送的内容与时机。
