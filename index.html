What your things are worth, and what they cost you

你的东西还值多少，又在花你多少

Cash and investments get tracked. The tens of thousands of pounds of things in your home never do — and they lose value every single day. Trove keeps that account.

现金和理财有人替你记账，家里那几十万的东西没有——而且它每天都在贬值。长物把这笔账记出来。

How it works

它怎么工作

  • Type a name, a price, and roughly when you bought it. The category is worked out from the name; you only pick one when it genuinely can't tell.
  • Known models fill themselves in. Recognise an iPhone or an A7 IV and its launch price, release date and depreciation curve all arrive at once.
  • Two numbers, not one. What an item is worth on paper, and what it would actually fetch if you sold it — those are different, and both matter.
  • Depreciation shown as spending. Most people have never seen how much their possessions quietly cost them each year.
  • The same model, run backwards. On the wishlist it tells you what something would really cost to own — which is often not what the price tag says.
  • 填名称、价格、大概什么时候买的。类型由名称自动判断，只有真判断不出来时才需要你挑一次。
  • 认识的型号会自己填好。识别出 iPhone 或 A7M4，发售价、发售日期和折旧曲线一次性全部带出来。
  • 给两个数字，不是一个。账面上值多少，和真拿去卖能拿回多少——这是两回事，而且都重要。
  • 把折旧当成支出来看。大多数人从没看清楚过，自己的东西每年悄悄花掉了多少钱。
  • 同一个模型反着跑。在心愿页，它告诉你一件东西真正的持有成本——往往和标价说的不是一回事。

Common questions

常见问题

How accurate are the valuations?

估值准不准？

They are model estimates, not appraisals. The curves are built from typical second-hand behaviour per category, with specific curves for well-known models. Reality varies with condition and local demand, so if a number looks wrong, open the item and tap Calibrate — enter what it would actually sell for and the curve is refitted around that point, keeping the category's shape.

它们是模型估算，不是专业评估。曲线基于各品类在二手市场的典型表现，知名型号还有各自的单品曲线。现实会随成色和当地行情变化，所以如果哪个数字看着不对，打开那件物品点「校准」——填入它实际能卖多少，曲线会围绕这个点重新拟合，同时保留品类原有的形状。

Where is my data stored?

我的数据存在哪？

Only on your device. There is no account, no server, and no networking code in the app at all. See the privacy policy.

只在你的设备上。没有账号，没有服务器，app 里根本没有联网代码。详见隐私政策。

Can I record something bought in another currency?

能记用其他货币买的东西吗？

Yes. Pick the currency next to the price when you record it. The amount is locked into your main currency using the exchange rate for the month you bought it — because what you actually spent is a historical fact and shouldn't drift. The item's current value, on the other hand, follows today's rate. You can set your main currency in Settings.

可以。录入时在价格旁边选货币即可。这笔金额会按你买入那个月份的汇率锁定到主货币——因为「当时花了多少」是历史事实，不该随汇率漂移。而这件物品的当前价值走的是今天的汇率。主货币在「设置」里改。

Why don't cheap things count toward my net worth?

便宜的东西为什么不计入净值？

Anything below the asset line goes into Light records instead — it's still tracked, and still shows a per-day cost, but it doesn't clutter the total. A ¥300 T-shirt in a ¥180,000 table just makes the number dirty. You can move the line in Settings.

低于资产线的东西会归入「轻记录」——仍然被记录，仍然显示日均成本，但不会把总数弄脏。一件 300 块的 T 恤混进 18 万的资产表里，只会让数字变脏。这条线可以在「设置」里调。

How do I switch language?

怎么切换语言？

Trove follows your system language. To set a different one just for this app, go to iOS Settings → Trove → Language, or use the shortcut in the app's own Settings screen.

长物跟随系统语言。想单独给这个 app 指定语言，去 iOS 的「设置 → 长物 → 语言」，或者用 app 设置页里的快捷入口。

Something I own isn't recognised.

我有件东西它没认出来。

The bundled model catalogue covers common electronics, cameras, lenses, consoles and a few other categories. Anything it doesn't know still works — it falls back to the category's curve, and you can calibrate it. Send the model name over and it can be added.

内置的型号目录覆盖常见的数码、相机、镜头、游戏机和另外几个品类。它不认识的东西照样能用——会回落到品类曲线，你也可以自己校准。把型号名发给我，可以加进去。
